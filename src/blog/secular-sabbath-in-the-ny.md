[I Need a Virtual Break. No, Really.](https://web.archive.org/web/20080302000000/http://www.nytimes.com/2008/03/02/fashion/02sabbath.html)  
  
Mark Bittman writes about the need to escape technology and connect to yourself once a week. I think it's a great idea.  
> In short, my name is Mark, and I'm a techno-addict. But after my airplane experience, I decided to do something about it. Thus began my "secular Sabbath" -- a term I found floating around on blogs -- a day a week where I would be free of screens, bells and beeps. An old-fashioned day not only of rest but of relief.

Escaping screens for 24 hours a week could only be a good thing for me.
