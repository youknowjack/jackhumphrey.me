A highlight of our vacation was definitely flying our 2-for-1 tickets on [eos](https://web.archive.org/web/20060822000000/http://eosairlines.com) from JFK to London Stansted. It's a all-first-class airline with fully-reclining seats. It was truly great, even though on the way back we were subject to the crazy security restrictions just imposed due to the foiled terrorist plot.
