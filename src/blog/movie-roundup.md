Time for Jack's movie roundup. I believe the last one I commented on was Closer. Some before and some after that...

+ Before Sunset: thumbs up, definitely goes near the top of my all-time best list

+ Garden State: thumbs up, enjoyable but a little bit forgettable

+ Sideways: thumbs up, fantastic movie, just see it

+ Incredibles: thumbs up, funny and original, Holly Hunter rocks

- I Heart Huckabees: thumbs down, really liked parts but overall left me kind of cold

+ House of Flying Daggers: thumbs up, I was riveted

+ Kinsey: thumbs up, a fascinating portrait of a man who changed the way we think about sex in this country; couldn't be more timely

- The Life Aquatic with Steve Ziszou: thumbs down, could have been great if Anderson had let the actors actually PLAY the characters

+ Hotel Rwanda: thumbs up, an important movie with amazing performances

+ Million Dollar Baby: thumbs up, great performances redeem the somewhat sappy story

+ In Good Company: thumbs up, Dennis Quaid and Topher Grace are hilarious in this actually very sincere movie about the ever-changing corporate world
