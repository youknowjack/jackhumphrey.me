After thinking about it for a long time, I got a new electric guitar over the weekend. I'm now the proud owner of a new Gibson [Les Paul Classic](http://www.gibson.com/Products/GibsonElectric/Gibson%20Electric%20Guitars/LesPaul/Classic/), in the beautiful Heritage Cherry Sunburst finish.

It's a beautiful guitar, and it sounds amazing. It's going to be hard to pick up the old Strat with the Les Paul sitting there. I took a [few pictures](http://picasaweb.google.com/leejack/Lespaul).
