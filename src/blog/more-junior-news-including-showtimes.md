The [Rural Route Film Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20060705122144/http://www.ruralroutefilms.com/home.htm) just posted the [schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20060714063726/http://www.ruralroutefilms.com/program_2006.htm#heydad) for this month's festival. [Junior](https://web.archive.org/web/20110207120625/http://juniorfilm.com) will be on at 9:30pm on Friday, July 28th, in a shorts program entitled "Hey Dad, The Ice Is Melting!" Their description of this program:
> This collection of short films explores relationships between people & the environment, people & animals, and fathers & sons. Black bears play in the local Canadian dump, Native Alaskans fight to preserve their way of life, **a Texas dad hits on high school chicks to impress his son**… Pine trees grow wild, but they also dominate motel signs.

Awesome.
